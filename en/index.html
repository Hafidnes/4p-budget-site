Real accounts.
A budget in four envelopes.

4P helps you track money coming in and going out, then compare your budget with the 60/20/10/10 benchmark.

Understand the methodGet help

60/20/10/10 is a starting point

Based on income

The allocation applies to income for the selected period. Your bank accounts can cover several envelopes; an envelope is not a bank account.

01 · Plan

60%

Fixed and essential expenses: housing, energy, insurance, transport…

02 · Provide

20%

Everyday food and other expenses you assign to this envelope.

03 · Play

10%

Leisure, clothing and the spending you choose for enjoyment.

04 · Protect

10%

Emergency savings, followed by the projects you want to prepare for.

An example with €2,000 in income

The initial benchmark gives €1,200 to Plan, €400 to Provide, €200 to Play and €200 to Protect.

What if essential expenses exceed 60%?

Plan takes priority. 4P highlights the difference and lets you prepare an adjustment. You choose how to divide the remainder and confirm the budget: the other percentages are not automatically imposed. If commitments exceed income, changing percentages does not remove the shortfall.

Three views, three purposes

1

Transactions

Record income, expenses and transfers. The bank account says where money comes in or goes out; the envelope describes its budget purpose.

2

Forecast

Prepare a month, simulate income and allocate envelopes. Saving a forecast does not record income in a bank account.

3

Dashboard

Read a summary of recorded activity, known scheduled items and the budget. A projection depends on the information you have entered.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a bank account for each P?

No. You can track several real accounts without dedicating each one to an envelope. For example, an expense can be paid from a checking account and categorized under Play.

Can I prepare several salaries or income sources?

Yes, income can be planned or recorded separately. Distinguish the income forecast used to prepare the budget from income actually recorded in an account.

Does allocating a budget move money?

No. Envelopes are a virtual allocation. 4P does not connect to your bank or execute bank transfers. A transfer between accounts is an operation you enter in the tracker.

Why does my bank balance differ from an envelope’s available amount?

The balance concerns money recorded in a real account. An envelope’s available amount concerns a period’s budget after expenses and reservations. These amounts measure different things.

What if my budget will not open?

Keep your original files and backups. If the recovery screen appears, review its available options before attempting recovery. Tell support which message you see and which app version you use.

What if a purchase is not confirmed?

In versions that offer subscriptions, use “Restore purchases” on the Subscription screen before trying again. Available terms, prices and trials are those shown in the app and by Apple. No purchases take place on this website.
