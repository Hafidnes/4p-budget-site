The 4P method

Four jobs for one income

Start from the household’s total income, one salary or several, and share it between four envelopes. The starting split is 60 / 20 / 10 / 10. You can adjust it, as long as the total is 100%.

Plan

60%

Fixed and essential bills: housing, energy, insurance, subscriptions, fuel, taxes, school costs.

Provide

20%

Everyday groceries, without the stress.

Play

10%

Leisure and clothing: enjoy it without guilt.

Protect

10%

Safety savings: emergency fund first, then projects, then the long term.

Try it with your numbers

Push bills above 60% of income to see how the method responds.

If Plan goes over

Essential bills come first, even above 60%. What remains is then shared between the other three envelopes by weight: half for Provide, a quarter for Play, a quarter for Protect. The app suggests, you confirm.

If Plan costs less

The surplus strengthens Protect. It does not automatically raise your leisure budget: you decide whether to move it.

If savings had to absorb a shock

Rebuilding Protect becomes next month’s priority. The method doesn’t judge you; it gets you back on your feet.

Envelopes are a split of your budget, not bank accounts. You can run everything from one account or several: allocating a budget never moves money.

Your data

Your accounts are nobody else’s business

A money app has to earn your trust first. Here is exactly what 4PMoney does, and what it doesn’t.

No bank connection

4PMoney never asks for your banking credentials and connects to no bank. You enter your transactions or import a statement you downloaded yourself.

Stored on your side

Your budget stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud space. We have no server holding your transactions.

Optional measurement, off by default

Anonymous usage statistics and diagnostics are only sent if you agree. They contain no amounts, descriptions or account names, and a single switch stops them.

Leave whenever you like

CSV export of your transactions and a full backup of your budget, at any time, including without a subscription.

4PMoney is a budget-tracking tool. It is not a bank or a payment service, and the app does not provide financial advice. Details are in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Does 4PMoney connect to my bank?

No. The app never asks for banking credentials. You enter transactions by hand or import a CSV, QIF or OFX statement downloaded from your bank’s website. The import is processed on your device.

Do I need to open four bank accounts?

No. The four envelopes are a split of your budget and can share a single account. You can also track several accounts; each transaction shows the account that pays and the envelope it belongs to.

Can I change the 60 / 20 / 10 / 10 split?

Yes. It is the method’s starting point. You set the four percentages however you like, as long as they add up to 100%.

What if my bills go above 60%?

Essential bills stay the priority. 4PMoney works out what is left and suggests sharing it between Provide, Play and Protect. Nothing is applied until you confirm.

Two of us manage the budget. Is that supported?

You can add household members, combine several incomes and split an expense between you. The budget syncs between devices signed in to the same iCloud account; sharing between two different iCloud accounts is not available today.

Which currencies and languages?

Amounts are in euros. The app is available in French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese, with Canadian and Brazilian variants.

What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?

It stays on your devices. You can still view it, export it and restore a backup. Only entering and editing are paused.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple Account settings, under Subscriptions. Access stays open until the end of the period already paid for.
