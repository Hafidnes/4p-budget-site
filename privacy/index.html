Privacy policy

Updated: 18 October 2026 · Abdelhafid YOUNES

Your budget stays on your devices and, if you use it, in your private iCloud space. Analytics and diagnostics are optional and off by default. Apple and RevenueCat verify subscriptions.

1. Who is responsible?

Abdelhafid YOUNES, the independent publisher of 4P Budget, is the controller for the processing described here. For privacy questions and requests, contact abdelhafid.younes@gmail.com.

2. Your budget, devices and iCloud

You enter income, expenses, accounts, descriptions, envelopes, scheduled items, goals and preferences to obtain the calculations and tracking you request. Bank accounts are entered manually: 4P does not connect to your bank or execute bank transfers.

Your budget is stored on your device. When iCloud is configured and available, the relevant data syncs in your private iCloud space across devices using the same Apple Account. The publisher does not operate a central server containing your budgets.

Exports, backups and shared files contain the data you choose to send. You choose their location and recipients. File sharing is not real-time sync between two Apple Accounts. The export feature does not send those files to analytics, diagnostic or subscription services.

3. Purchases, trials and restoration

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat verifies purchases, trials and access rights and enables restoration. It processes a pseudonymous technical identifier, products and transactions, their status, receipts and the metadata needed for verification. Its SDK may contact the service at launch independently of optional analytics consent. It does not receive budget amounts or descriptions. 4P does not receive your payment card number.

A local pending-purchase record stores a reference date and the type of verification to resume, separately for each edition and technical identity. It contains neither a budget nor proof of payment. It is removed when access confirmation, restoration or explicit cancellation resolves the attempt; an error can delay resolution. Removing this record or a RevenueCat profile does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Budget viewing, export and recovery remain available after a subscription ends.

4. Optional analytics — PostHog

Analytics is off by default. You can enable or withdraw it in Settings → Privacy, separately on each device. Allowed events cover app launch, the screen viewed and a technical test. They include the app version, edition, environment and a pseudonymous installation identifier.

Unapproved properties, including budget amounts, descriptions and account names, are filtered before transmission. Person profiles, automatic interaction capture and session recording are disabled. The project uses PostHog Cloud EU, hosted in Frankfurt, with IP anonymization enabled and event geolocation disabled. An IP address is still needed for network transport; this does not mean providers process no network metadata.

5. Optional diagnostics — Sentry

Diagnostics is also off by default. With your consent, Sentry receives scrubbed errors and technical stack traces to investigate faults, together with the app version and environment. Filtering removes user data, requests, free text and budget information from the transmitted signal. Session recording, screenshots and automatic log collection are disabled. The configured ingestion endpoint is European.

Withdrawing either consent stops new uploads for that purpose; it does not automatically delete events already received. You can request deletion using the contact above. Declining either option does not prevent you from using budgets or purchases.

6. Permissions and support

Authorized notifications provide reminders. If biometric locking is enabled, authentication is performed by Apple’s system; 4P does not receive your fingerprint or biometric model. You can change permissions in system settings.

If you email support, your address and the content you send are processed to answer your request. The support mailbox is hosted by Google/Gmail. Avoid sending an entire budget, passwords or bank details; redact private information in screenshots.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Budget tracking, the sync you use, exports and access verification are necessary to perform the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Optional analytics and diagnostics rely on consent (6(1)(a)). Support and service security rely on the legitimate interest in answering requests and preventing incidents (6(1)(f)), subject to your rights. Processing required by law relies on Article 6(1)(c). 4P does not use your budget for advertising, sell it or make decisions with legal effects based on a financial profile.

8. Retention and deletion

Budgets and files: data remains in the locations you use until you delete it: the app, your iCloud space, backups and exports. Delete items in the app, manage iCloud copies through Apple settings and delete exported files separately. Uninstalling the app does not delete all these copies. The publisher cannot remotely delete a budget it cannot access.

PostHog analytics: the publisher sets a maximum retention of twelve months from each event. Expired-event deletion is followed up through periodic reviews; withdrawing consent does not automatically erase prior events. PostHog’s query window is not a data deletion mechanism.

Sentry diagnostics: error events follow the Sentry plan’s retention of thirty to ninety days, depending on the plan; 4P does not archive them separately.

RevenueCat: information needed for access remains available during the subscription and for restoration. The publisher plans deletion no later than twenty-four months after the last entitlement expires, provided there is no new purchase activity, dispute or obligation justifying specific retention. A later restoration can recreate a profile from Apple’s history. Apple applies its own retention rules to its transaction records.

Support: 4P correspondence is kept while a request is handled and for no more than twenty-four months after closure, except for information needed for a legal obligation or dispute. Only necessary information is retained for the applicable period in that case. Provider deletion requests may be asynchronous; technical backups follow the provider’s deletion cycle and are not used for routine analytics.

9. Providers and international transfers

Apple provides system services, iCloud and payments; RevenueCat, Inc. handles subscriptions; PostHog provides optional analytics; Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) provides optional diagnostics; Google hosts support email; and GitHub, Inc. hosts these pages. Access by the publisher and these providers is limited to the functions described. The data is not all combined in a single service.

European collection endpoints at PostHog and Sentry do not rule out all processing or access from other countries. Providers or subprocessors may operate in the United States, in particular. Their data processing agreements describe recipients and mechanisms for transfers outside the European Economic Area, including European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. You can request details and a copy of the applicable safeguards using the contact at the top of this page. This policy does not claim that all processing stays within the European Union.

10. These web pages

This static site contains no forms, analytics scripts, external fonts or payment tools. GitHub Pages processes visitor IP addresses for hosting security. Visiting these pages does not send your budget to the site. External services linked here are contacted only when you follow a link.

11. Your rights and updates

Subject to GDPR conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Email abdelhafid.younes@gmail.com, mentioning 4P and the service concerned. A technical identifier or proportionate information may be needed to locate pseudonymous data; we do not ask for your Apple password or a complete budget. We respond within one month; for complex requests, any reasoned extension is notified within that first month. If data cannot be located without sufficient identification, we explain this.

You may complain to the CNIL or your local supervisory authority. Changes in practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date. New consent will be requested where necessary.
